# Data availability statement

*(Elsevier "Research Data" Option C: deposit and link the data/code, or state
why it cannot be shared. This text also appears as the Data availability
section in the manuscript.)*

The raw CICIoT2023 dataset is distributed under the Canadian Institute for
Cybersecurity (CIC) license and is available from the original providers; we
therefore do not redistribute the raw data. All code, processing pipelines, and
the hash-chained result manifests required to reproduce every figure and number
in this paper are archived at:

- Public release: https://github.com/feli-santos/rl-iot-defense-system/releases/tag/v0.8.1
- Project repository: https://github.com/feli-santos/rl-iot-defense-system

CICIoT2023 is cited as a dedicated `[dataset]` reference in `refs.bib`
(`CICIoT2023Dataset`) and as the associated peer-reviewed dataset paper
(Neto et al., 2023).
