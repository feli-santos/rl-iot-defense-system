The figure's claim is **monotonicity of PPO mean reward in `p`**, not absolute level: PPO at `p=1.0` reaches +2047, exceeding the Phase-6 oracle ceiling +1624 because the high-`p` cells operate in a strictly easier MDP than the Phase-6 default `p_defender_deescalation = 0.0` (the Phase-6 ceiling is computed under that default; F10 cells perturb the MDP itself).
