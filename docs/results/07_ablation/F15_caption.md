**F15 — OOD-class robustness.** Mean episodic reward of every Phase-6 policy under each held-out attack class (DDoS-HTTP_Flood, Mirai-udpplain, VulnerabilityScan, XSS), with the env's `RealizationEngine.allowed_indices` restricted to that class's row indices.
