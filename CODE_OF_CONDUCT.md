# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free
environment for everyone involved in this research codebase — regardless of
background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards

**Expected:**

- Use welcoming, inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the research.

**Unacceptable:**

- Harassment, trolling, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional
  academic or engineering setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository
maintainer via GitHub issues (for non-sensitive matters) or the security
contact below (for sensitive reports). All reports are reviewed and handled
with discretion.

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in any public
space where an individual is representing the project or its community.
